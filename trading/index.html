    HOW A SIGNAL IS MADE
    
    
    
      Every 60 seconds each contract is re-scored by six specialist agents. Each one returns a vote from −1 (strong short) to +1 (strong long) with a weight. The Chief Strategist blends the votes into a score from −100 to +100. Above +30 is LONG, below −30 is SHORT, and anything between is HOLD. The Risk Manager can veto any trade when the structural stop sits too close to liquidation for that coin's leverage (15x / 10x / 5x / 4x).

    
      Entry: MARKET when price is within 0.35 ATR of the 15m fast EMA; otherwise a LIMIT at the 15m EMA21 pull-back. Stop: beyond the 3-hour swing or 1.2× the 1h ATR, whichever is wider, capped at 45% of the liquidation distance. Targets: TP1 1.5R (close 40%, move SL to entry), TP2 2.5R (close 35%), TP3 4R (run 25%).

    WHALE TRADES · ALL 23 COINS —
    
    
    Individual large taker trades straight from Binance's own public trade feed for BTC/ETH/XRP/SOL/LTC/BNB — not on-chain wallet transfers, but a free, same-exchange proxy for aggressive big-ticket flow. Each coin's running buy-minus-sell total among these whale trades only (its "whale CVD") feeds a small, capped piece of the Derivatives Analyst's vote.

    Every forced liquidation Binance's futures engine reports, exchange-wide, in real time. A cluster on one coin is flagged as a cascade below — it's shown for awareness only and never moves a score, since forced selling can mark capitulation near a low just as easily as it can keep feeding a downtrend.
